Explore the zoo

Allow 3 hours · leave via Rail Gate

Simple loop from Rail Gate: Lion Gorge (tiger/lions/snow leopard) → Wild Sea (seals/penguins) → Forest of Wonder (orangutans/tiger) → Gorilla Rainforest → giraffes → back to Rail Gate. Don't force every exhibit.

Use the zoo map for exact paths, toilets, water and food. Stops at animals break up the walking. At noon, start steering everyone towards the Rail Gate.

ACMI: free interactive exhibition

10am–5pm · free

At Federation Square, enter ACMI from the Fed Square plaza and head to the Ground Floor. Visit The Story of the Moving Image: playable videogames, film props and interactive exhibits. No ticket for this exhibition.
